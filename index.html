About Subham Housing Hostel

We provide a safe, nurturing, and educational environment for school students to grow academically and personally.

Our Mission & Vision

At Subham Housing Hostel, we believe that a child's environment plays a crucial role in their overall development. Our mission is to create a home-like atmosphere where students can focus on their studies while developing essential life skills.

Located in the peaceful area of Unkarantipur, Palamandap District, Dhenkanal, Odisha, we offer a perfect blend of modern amenities and traditional values to ensure holistic growth.
